The problem with spec-driven development

One document, four jobs

The spec has to be intent, definition of done, workflow and technical context at once, written however the author felt that day.

A complete spec cannot be written up front

OpenAI's Symphony spec (2,169 lines) was distilled from a system that already ran. It was written last, not first.

The agent fills every hole

Gaps become agent decisions, at volume. Teams feel fast while being wrong; a 2025 METR trial found developers slower with AI but sure they were faster.

The cost does not disappear. Rework and wasted tokens move down the line to the client's invoice.

Source: Ahuja (May 2026). His own example: three days of rework, about $985 in tokens, from one good spec left unattended.

Key point: the holes are not a tooling defect. They come from asking people to specify everything before it exists.
