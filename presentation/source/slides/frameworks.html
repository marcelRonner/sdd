Current SDD frameworks: harnesses, not methods

FrameworkBackerApproachTypical fit

Over 30 frameworks exist. All structure the spec-to-code workflow; none decides who owns intent and the definition of done.

As of October 2026. Sources: ssojet.com, softwareseni.com, codemyspec.com, github.com/cameronsjo/spec-compare, developertoolkit.ai.
